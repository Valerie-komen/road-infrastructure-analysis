Data Quality Notes

What the cleaning pass caught — worth a slide of its own

!

68 rows (~10%) had a budget-parsing bug in the source "clean" table — "Ksh.4,359,000,000" was read as 0.4359. Fixed by stripping the currency prefix before parsing, recovering ~KES 112B in understated budget.

!

22 projects showed apparent over-disbursement (~155% of budget) — traced to disbursements filed under two different spellings of the same project code, not real overspending. Flagged for finance reconciliation rather than silently merged.

!

158 raw county spellings collapsed to Kenya's real 47 via a validated join against a reference table, catching 2 misspellings (Monbasa → Mombasa, Kissumu → Kisumu) along the way.
